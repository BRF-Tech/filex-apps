# e-Signature

Sign documents yourself or ask other people to sign them - inside filex, with no external service.
Both sides of a signature are the same screen, step by step.

- **Sign** a PDF yourself: place the boxes, fill them in, say where the result goes.
- **Request signatures** from one or more people: who signs, in what order, which boxes each of them
  fills, how long they have, and what happens when it is done.
- **Every box has a name.** The signer fills a short form of named boxes instead of hunting across a
  page; the name is in the box, the form, the audit trail and the PDF's own field.
- **A signer is an identity, not an address.** A name, an e-mail address, or both. Somebody with no
  address still gets a link and a PIN.
- **A receipt for every signer** the moment their signature lands: their certificate and both
  SHA-256 fingerprints.
- **Verify** any PDF, signed here or anywhere else: who signed, which authority stands behind them,
  and whether the signature covers the whole file.
- **The file can be frozen** read-only for everyone while signatures are collected.
- **PDFs only, and no engine.** To sign an office document, convert it to PDF first (the Convert app
  does it).

Five languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German and French. Spanish, German and French are
AI-translated, awaiting review by a native speaker - corrections welcome.
